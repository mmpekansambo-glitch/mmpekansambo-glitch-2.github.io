---
name: Portfolio contact privacy
description: The user's preference for how visitors should contact her through the portfolio.
---

Do not display the user's personal email on public portfolio pages. Use her LinkedIn profile as the public contact route.

**Why:** The user decided not to make her email public and chose LinkedIn as the alternative.

**How to apply:** Keep both the React and Jekyll contact pages free of email addresses and `mailto:` links; link visitors to LinkedIn instead.
