<section class="prose-section" aria-labelledby="about-me">
  <h2 id="about-me">Where I’m coming from</h2>
  <p>I’m an MBA candidate at the University of California, Berkeley, Haas School of Business. Before business school, I served as an investment banking associate, working with a diverse and talented team to provide financial advisory and capital-raising services to clients across the Technology, Media, and Telecom landscape.</p>
  <p>I graduated from Stanford University with a B.A. in Economics and a minor in Creative Writing. That combination helped me develop analytical, critical, and creative ways of thinking — skills I continue to bring to the work I do.</p>
</section>

<section class="prose-section" aria-labelledby="what-matters">
  <h2 id="what-matters">What matters to me</h2>
  <p>I’m passionate about the financial education of women and girls around the world, especially in my home country of Zambia. I’m also interested in promoting women’s participation in STEM, agriculture, literature, and African politics.</p>
</section>
